# AJ Wells - Software Developer

## Introduction

I'm AJ Wells, a seasoned Front End Developer skilled in React, React Native, TypeScript, and JavaScript. My journey began in 2004 with HTML and CSS, evolving through technologies like Adobe Flash. After a hiatus, I re-entered the tech scene in 2021, graduating from a Full Stack Development Bootcamp. This experience refined my proficiency in contemporary development tools and frameworks, fueling my passion for crafting engaging web and mobile solutions.

## Professional Experience

From August 2021 to July 2023, I led Front End Design for a cattle management company, creating applications in React and React Native and expanding my expertise in technologies like TypeScript and MongoDB.


## Current Focus & Aspirations

Since August 2023, I've continued expanding my development skills through personal projects and independent study. My work includes React Native applications, backend development with C#/.NET, Supabase, and Firebase, and a Zork-inspired text adventure developed with Python, Flask, and JavaScript.

**2026 & Beyond:** In Fall 2026, I returned to North Dakota State University (NDSU) to strengthen my computer science foundation. What began as a plan to complete a few courses and earn a certificate has evolved into a larger commitment. I'm now officially pursuing a **Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, with a minor in Software Development**.

Alongside my coursework, I'm involved with **Bison Robotics / NASA Lunabotics**, gaining experience with robotics, programming, and collaborative engineering projects.

My long-term interests are focused on developing better graphical user interfaces for software applications and video games, combining my front-end development experience with a deeper understanding of software engineering and system architecture. Eventually, I'd also like to explore game development further or share my experience by teaching programming and technology to younger generations.
